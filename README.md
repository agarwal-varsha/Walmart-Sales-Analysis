# Walmart Sales Analysis
**By Varsha Agarwal**

## Overview
Exploratory data analysis of Walmart sales data using Python and Pandas to uncover 
revenue drivers across branches, product lines, and customer segments.

## Business Question
Which product lines, branches, and customer segments drive the most revenue — 
and where should Walmart focus to improve performance?

## Dataset
- 1000 transactions across 3 branches (A, B, C)
- January to March 2019
- 17 variables including product line, customer type, payment method, and ratings

## Key Findings
1. **Health & Beauty has a pricing problem** — highest customer satisfaction but lowest revenue
2. **Branch B is underperforming** — lowest revenue and lowest customer rating
3. **January is peak month** — February drops by 16%
4. **Female customers drive more revenue** — especially in Food & Beverages
5. **Customer satisfaction does NOT drive revenue** — correlation is only -0.036
